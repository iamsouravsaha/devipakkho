HOW TO OPEN THIS WEBSITE

1. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All".
2. Open the extracted folder "maaaschen".
3. Double-click "index.html". It opens in your browser.

Note: The YouTube songs may not play when you double-click the file.
They play properly when the site is online (GitHub) or opened with
VS Code "Live Server" (Go Live).

TO ADD SONGS
Open js/main.js in Notepad, find "const playlists", and add one
YouTube link per line in each list (comma after every link).
